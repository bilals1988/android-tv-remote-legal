Remote control and compatibility

The Android phone app uses Android TV Remote v2 to control compatible Android TV and Google TV devices. The phone and TV must be able to reach each other on the local network. Pairing normally requires a PIN shown on the TV. TV software, Remote Service versions, the network and the active TV app can affect discovery, pairing, remote commands, text entry and voice. TVs without the supported remote service cannot be controlled by the remote.

The app is independent. Google, television manufacturers, and streaming providers do not sponsor or endorse it. App shortcuts request that a TV open a supported link. Responses vary by TV and app version. Streaming services may require separate installation, an account, a subscription, or regional access.

TV Art and the free trial

TV Art receiver compatibility is separate from remote control compatibility. In builds where casting is enabled, Google Cast requires a compatible Cast receiver. Experimental DLNA requires a compatible MediaRenderer and image playback support. A connected or paired TV may support neither. A completed fetch does not confirm the image appeared on the screen. Photo transfer uses unencrypted HTTP on the local network.

Where available, the free trial lets you start one session with the designated trial artwork. The app uses the trial when a receiver completes a fetch after accepting the image load. Opening the gallery, discovering receivers or a failed attempt does not by itself use the trial. Once the used session ends or can no longer resume, additional sessions require verified Lifetime Pro. The trial record is installation-local and may reset if app data is cleared or the app is reinstalled. Personal photos and the full casting gallery require Pro where the relevant features are enabled.
